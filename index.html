A gentle check-in

What's been on your mind lately?

You don't always need the right words for it. If something has been feeling heavy or hard to carry lately, that's something a psychologist can help you work through.

  • Your mind won't switch off, even when you're exhausted
  • Small things feel overwhelming lately
  • You're managing fine on the outside, but feeling low on the inside
  • Relationships feel harder than they used to
  • Academic or work pressure feels heavier than usual
  • Stress has quietly become part of everyday life

If even one of these feels familiar, it might be time to talk to a therapist and I'd be glad to be that person.

About

Hi, I'm Shruti

I believe everyone deserves a space where they can simply be themselves, without judgment or pressure to have it all figured out. Most of the time, figuring it out is exactly what we're there to do together.

I work closely with young adults, college students, and early career professionals, many of whom seem completely fine on the outside while quietly carrying anxiety that won't settle, slow burnout, or self-doubt that creeps in no matter how much they've achieved. If you've been holding everything together for a while and you're just tired, that's reason enough to reach out. You don't need a bigger crisis to justify asking for support.

I like to think of therapy a little like sitting beside someone who's learning to drive again after a long time away from the wheel. In the beginning, I might help hold things steady through the parts that feel unfamiliar or overwhelming, so you're not facing them alone. But it's still your car and your journey and slowly, at your own pace, you'll find yourself taking the wheel back until you don't need me sitting beside you anymore.

"Healing isn't about becoming someone new. It's about coming home to yourself."

Services

How I can help

Therapy looks different for everyone, and these are some of the areas I work with most often, though we'll always shape our sessions around what you actually need.

Anxiety & Stress

When persistent worry, overthinking, or physical tension begin to weigh on daily life, we work together to understand the root causes and cultivate a steady, grounding sense of calm.

Burnout & Exhaustion

For when you have been pushing through fatigue for too long. We will gently explore what is depleting your energy and rebuild sustainable rhythms for both work and rest.

Life Transitions

Whether navigating a career shift, relocation, graduation, or a changing relationship, you can feel disoriented by major milestones. Therapy offers a reassuring space to regain your footing.

Relationship Dynamics

Untangling recurring conflicts, communication barriers, attachment patterns, or the quiet loneliness that can arise even when surrounded by others.

Academics and Early Career Pressures

Managing performance expectations, identity shifts, and the uncertainty inherent to this stage of life. You do not have to carry that weight alone.

Self-Esteem & Imposter Syndrome

Addressing that quiet fear of falling short or being exposed, even when your accomplishments show otherwise. We will nurture an authentic self-worth that no longer depends on constant validation.

Healthy Boundaries

Learning to say no without guilt, letting go of the need to overextend for others, and establishing clear, compassionate limits that honor your well-being.

Emotional Regulation

For moments when feelings build up quietly and spill over, or when spiraling thoughts feel overwhelming. We will develop practical ways to process emotions safely before they become consuming.

Procrastination & Overwhelm

When tasks accumulate and deadlines spark paralysis rather than momentum, we will break patterns down into gentle, actionable steps that feel truly manageable.

What to expect

What happens next?

Beginning therapy can feel daunting, but taking the first step is an act of gentle courage. Here is a transparent look at how we will begin together:

  1. 1

    Submit the therapy registration form

    A brief, thoughtful registration form. Take your time and share only what feels safe and comfortable for you right now.

  2. 2

    Shruti contacts you

    I will personally read through your note and write back within 24 hours with my open slots and answer any initial questions you might have about our work together.

  3. 3

    Schedule & onboarding

    Choose a time that fits your routine. We will confirm your slot, and you will receive an intake form and informed consent to read through and sign before we begin.

  4. 4

    Your first therapy session

    We then meet online for 50 minutes via Google Meet!

Sessions are 50 minutes long and conducted virtually via a private, secure Google Meet link. You will need a stable internet connection, a quiet and private space where you feel comfortable speaking openly, and earphones for better clarity.

I do not offer free phone consultations. Instead, the registration form allows me to understand your concerns upfront and ensure our clinical fit before we begin. You are also welcome to ask any preliminary questions via email before confirming your first full session.
Each 50-minute individual session is fee-based. Payment is required in advance to confirm your scheduled slot. For clients based in India, payments are accepted via UPI, Net Banking, or standard online transfers. International clients can pay via secure digital transfer links.
