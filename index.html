You don't need a diagnosis to reach out. Many people come to therapy simply because something feels off — and that's enough.

  • Small things feel overwhelming lately

  • Relationships feel harder than they used to

  • Stress and burnout have become your normal

If even one of these resonates, talking to someone can help. You don't have to figure this out alone.

About

Hi, I'm Shruti

I started Buoyant Psyche because I believe everyone deserves a space where they can be fully themselves — without judgment, without rushing, and without having to have it all figured out first.

The name Buoyant Psyche reflects what I hope therapy feels like: not drowning in your thoughts, but finding enough support to stay afloat — and eventually, to move forward with more ease.

I work primarily with young adults, college students, and early-career professionals navigating anxiety, life transitions, relationship challenges, and the quiet exhaustion that comes from holding everything together.

In our sessions, you can expect warmth, honesty, and a pace that respects where you are today. I won't fix you — because you're not broken. We'll work together, at your speed.

Services

How I can help

Therapy is collaborative and tailored to you. These are some of the areas I work with most often.

Anxiety & Stress

When worry, overthinking, or constant tension start affecting your daily life, therapy can help you understand patterns and find steadier ground.

Burnout & Exhaustion

For when you've been pushing through for too long. We'll explore what's draining you and how to rebuild sustainable ways of living and working.

Life Transitions

Career changes, relocation, graduation, or relationship shifts — big changes can unsettle even the most capable people.

Relationships

Navigating conflict, communication patterns, attachment, or the loneliness that can exist even close to others.

College & Early Career

Academic pressure, identity questions, and the uncertainty of early adulthood — you don't have to navigate this decade alone.

Online Therapy

Secure video sessions from wherever you feel comfortable — ideal if travel, schedule, or preference makes in-person difficult.

Session details

  • Duration: 50 minutes

What to expect

What happens next?

Reaching out is the hardest step. Here's exactly what comes after — no surprises.

  1. 1

    Submit the form

    A short booking form — less than two minutes. Share as much or as little as you're comfortable with.

  2. 2

    Shruti contacts you

    I'll reach out within 1–2 business days via your preferred contact method.

  3. 3

    Brief consultation

    A free 15–20 minute call to understand your needs, answer questions, and see if we're a good fit.

  4. 4

    Schedule your session

    We'll find a time that works for you — no pressure to commit before you're ready.

  5. 5

    Your first therapy session

    We go at your pace. There's no script — just a safe space to begin.
